How your answers are used

  • This survey is part of a school project by students at St Mary's Edenderry for the Irish Angus Schools Competition.
  • Taking part is voluntary. Your name and county are optional, and you can leave them blank to take part anonymously.
  • Your answers will only be used for this project. We will not sell them or share them with anyone else, and we will not use them for marketing.
  • Results are reported as totals and percentages only. No names or individual farms will be identified in anything we publish or present.
  • Responses are stored securely, and only the project team can see them. They will be deleted when the project is finished.
  • If you would like your response removed, or have any questions, contact Sean Monahan on 085 1287997 or seanmonahan23@stmarysedenderry.ie.
